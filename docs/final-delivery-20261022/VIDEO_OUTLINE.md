This is a recording plan. The published walkthrough is https://youtu.be/fRGUjTdF3nk (YouTube Unlisted). A 60–90 second edit is suggested, not required by the rules. No mainnet footage, link or transaction is invented. DoraHacks was not submitted.

Record the real authorized payment once. Preserve its original footage and source-build identity; an edit may remove waiting but must not fabricate wallet confirmation, transactions, labels or verification. The later showcase build can re-verify the earlier payment if the initiating build and subsequent differences are accurately recorded.

Keep chain, amounts, shortened addresses plus copyable links, transaction status and verification labels legible. Hide passwords, recovery phrases, tokens, personal email and unrelated wallet balances. Do not ask anyone to reveal a secret for recording. Browser mock data, Anvil and offline fixtures cannot stand in for mainnet evidence.

Before delivery, play the final file, check narration against the retained evidence, verify public playback without login, and enter the actual video URL in [submission-state.json](submission-state.json). Do not re-transfer funds merely to fill a missing shot; re-use genuine footage or show the verified Explorer/receipt record with an accurate caption.
